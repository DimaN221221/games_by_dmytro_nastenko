Precision platformer

Robot Mil

A hardcore platformer where you guide Mil, a little robot, through level after level of saws, lasers and spikes. Every chapter has its own location and its own traps. It's time to test your nerves.

Released
October 10, 2025
Platform
Windows
Languages
English, Ukrainian, German, French, Italian, Polish, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional)
View on Steam Wishlist on Epic Games Store

  • 4 chapters, 56 levels

    14 levels per chapter, each chapter with its own setting and obstacles.

  • Normal and Hard mode

    Same locations, more obstacles and far less room for error.

  • Secret chapter

    Collect the puzzles hidden in levels to unlock “Another Way”.

  • Challenges

    A whole chapter as one long level. No checkpoints.

  • Practice mode

    Drop a checkpoint anywhere and drill the part that keeps beating you.

  • Level Maker

    Build your own levels and share them through Steam Workshop.

Top-down shooter

Bullet Vendetta

Bullet Vendetta is a dynamic top-down shooter about the fight against the mafia that controls the city.

Release
To be announced
Platform

Wishlist on Steam Play the demo

  • Intense shootouts

    Bullet Vendetta is a fast-paced action shooter packed with intense and thrilling shootouts.

  • Revenge

    Betrayed by the mafia and having served time in prison, the protagonist finally gets his chance for revenge after years behind bars.

  • Mafia districts

    The mafia controls various districts of the city, and it’s up to you to clear them of criminals. Use a variety of firearms to fight your way through crowds of enemies and survive in unequal battles.

  • Quick decisions

    Make quick decisions, adapt to dangerous situations, and fight your way through a hail of bullets. Free the city from mafia control and carry out the revenge that has been burning inside the main character for many years.
