Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what data is involved when you play Robot Mil or Bullet Vendetta (including its demo) and when you visit this website. All of them are made by Dmytro Nastenko, an independent developer (“I”, “me”).

The short version: I do not run any servers, I do not collect analytics, and I do not sell or share your data. Your progress is stored on your own computer. The stores you play through (Steam, Epic Games Store) handle your account, achievements and similar features under their own privacy policies.

1. Data in the games

The games save your progress, settings, best times, death counters and unlocked achievements in local files on your computer. On Windows they are kept in %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Dmytro Nastenko\. The games do not send these files anywhere.

Levels you create in the Robot Mil Level Maker are also stored locally until you choose to publish them.

The games contain no advertising and no analytics or tracking code of my own.

2. Steam and Epic Games Store

Steam

The Steam versions use Steamworks, provided by Valve Corporation, for:

  • Achievements — unlocks are recorded on your Steam account.
  • Rich Presence — your Steam friends can see what you are doing in the game (for example, which level you are on).
  • Steam Workshop (Robot Mil) — when you publish a level, it is uploaded to the Workshop together with your Steam profile name, and other players can see and download it. You can remove your levels from the Workshop at any time.

Valve processes this data under the Steam Privacy Policy. As the developer I only see aggregated reports that Valve provides (such as sales and achievement percentages), not your personal data.

Epic Games Store

The Epic Games Store version of Robot Mil uses Epic Online Services and Epic Account Services. When you start the game from the Epic Games Launcher, it signs you in with your Epic account. With your consent, the game receives your Epic account ID and display name (basic profile), and Epic creates a game-specific user ID for you. This is used only to record and show your achievements, including in the Epic overlay.

Epic processes this data under the Epic Games Privacy Policy. I do not store your Epic account data anywhere outside Epic's services.

3. Unity engine

The games are built with Unity. The engine may send anonymous technical information about your device (such as operating system, processor, graphics card and memory) to Unity Technologies. This does not include your name or account details. See the Unity Game Player Privacy Policy.

4. This website

This is a static website. It sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics and no forms.

  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address when you visit, for security and to deliver the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request with your IP address to Google. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Game trailers are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press “Watch the trailer”; after that, YouTube (Google) may process your IP address and store data in your browser to play the video. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Links to Steam, YouTube, TikTok and Telegram take you to those services, where their own policies apply.

5. Emails you send me

If you write to me, I receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. I use it only to reply to you and keep it only as long as needed for that conversation.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Local data: delete the folder mentioned in section 1, or uninstall the game, to erase your saves.
  • Store data: achievements, Workshop items and account data are managed in your Steam or Epic account settings. Those companies handle requests to access or delete it.
  • Epic sign-in: you can revoke the game's access in your Epic account under Apps and Accounts.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you. Because I do not hold player data myself, most such requests go to Valve or Epic, but you can always contact me and I will help.

7. Children

I do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Store accounts, and the age rules for them, are handled by Steam and the Epic Games Store.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
